## Completed Tasks

We managed to finish all of our tasks in Sprint 3, which is awesome! Here’s a quick summary of what we got done:

### 1. **Storing Workouts in a Database**
   - **Story:** Now users can log their workouts, and everything gets saved in a database. This means they can keep track of their progress over time!
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can input details like exercises, sets, and reps, and view their workout history anytime.

### 2. **Storing Email and Password in a Database**
   - **Story:** We improved the login system by storing users’ credentials in a secure text file. This makes logging in quicker and safer.
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** User login details are saved and can be retrieved efficiently whenever needed.

### 3. **Preset Workouts with Specific Exercises**
   - **Story:** We added buttons for preset workouts, so users can log common routines (like "Push Workout") with just one click. No need to fill in everything manually!
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** Clicking on a preset workout button automatically fills in the exercises, sets, and reps.

### 4. **Enhanced Calorie and Macronutrient Input**
   - **Story:** Users can now log their macronutrients (proteins, fats, carbs) along with calories, making nutrition tracking more accurate.
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can track their daily intake of calories and macronutrients to stay on top of their diet goals.

### 5. **Page Customization**
   - **Story:** Users can now pick from different color themes to customize the website to their liking. You can choose colors like Blue, Green, Red, and more!
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** The website updates its color scheme based on the user’s selection, giving it a personalized feel.

## Practices to Continue

### 1. **Regular Team Check-ins and Stand-ups**
   - Our daily check-ins and stand-up meetings were key to staying organized and making sure everything was on track. We’ll definitely keep doing this to maintain good communication within the team.

### 2. **Collaborative Learning and Knowledge Sharing**
   - We’ve been learning new things as a team, from JavaScript to database stuff. This collaborative approach really helped us solve problems and get things done faster. We’ll keep this up in future projects!

### 3. **Code Reviews and Testing**
   - Reviewing each other’s code and testing early in the sprint really helped us spot bugs before they became bigger issues. This has been super helpful, and we’ll continue doing code reviews to maintain high-quality work.

---

## New Practices to Consider

### 1. **Earlier Integration of Frontend and Backend**
   - We noticed that integrating the frontend and backend earlier in the sprint makes things run smoother. We’ll try to do this more often in future sprints to avoid last-minute integration issues.

### 2. **Documentation Updates Throughout the Sprint**
   - Updating documentation as we go rather than at the end of the sprint helped us keep everything up-to-date. This saved us a lot of time and stress, and we’ll continue doing this moving forward.

---

## Practices to Avoid

### 1. **Waiting Too Long to Address Feature Dependencies**
   - Sometimes, we found ourselves waiting until later in the sprint to address features that depend on each other. This led to some delays. Moving forward, we’ll try to identify and address dependencies earlier to keep things on track.
