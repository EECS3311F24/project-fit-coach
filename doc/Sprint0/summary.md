In today's fitness-driven world, a website that tracks everything from calories to workout progress is a must-have for athletes and fitness lovers. A platform that lets users track their food, macronutrients, and exercise progress would be very useful. It would attract a wide range of people, like bodybuilders, powerlifters, CrossFitters, athletes, beginners, and even fitness coaches. With its easy-to-use features, people can track their progress, and reach their fitness goals faster. This would make the website a complete tool for improving health, fitness, and overall performance.

## Beginners & Athletes

Beginners would benefit from the guidance in both workouts and caloric intake to help them get started on the right foot. Additionally, streak tracking would be available to help users maintain consistency. A built-in calendar would provide a visual representation of their workout history, allowing them to review past workouts and ensure consistency in their routine. The platform would serve as a guide, providing access to the exercise library and offering caloric intake suggestions that align with their fitness goals. ## Bodybuilders & Powerlifters

Bodybuilders would benefit from precise tracking of macronutrients and hypertrophy-focused workouts, while powerlifters could monitor their strength gains and progressive overload. CrossFitters, with their focus on both endurance and strength, would find value in tracking high-intensity training. They could design a routine with a focus on hypertrophy, strength, or endurance, and adjust the program as they progress. The website would include a progressive overload recommendation system, which suggests incremental increases in weight or repetitions based on previous workouts. Bodybuilders could use the website to track their hypertrophy by logging their weight and exercise routines while monitoring their caloric intake to ensure they are eating enough to support muscle growth. Additionally, users could log their daily food intake, tracking calories and macronutrients to achieve their fitness goals.

## Other Functionalities

The website will offer customizable themes and templates and an exercise library. Users can also create and log custom exercises, ensuring flexibility in their training routines. Coaches could use the website to see their client’s workouts and meal plans. 

## Key uses

The fitness website allows users to add exercises to specific workout days. It features a progressive overload system, adjusting weight and reps for optimal progress. A tier system ranks users based on the weight lifted, while streak tracking helps maintain consistency. The website could generate a customizable caloric intake plan according to the user's lifestyle and goals
