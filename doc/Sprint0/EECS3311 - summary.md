### **Existing Products**

1. **Strong (Gym Workout Tracker)** This app focuses on tracking workout progress but lacks nutrition tracking. It primarily appeals to bodybuilders and strength trainers but limits customization options for workouts and themes.  
2. **RepCount (Gym Workout Tracker**) Similar to Strong, RepCount tracks workouts but does not include any nutritional tracking, making it less usable. The app also targets strength trainers but lacks customizable features.  
3. **MyFitnessPal (Nutrition Tracker**) Primarily focused on calorie counting and basic exercise logging, MyFitnessPal does not provide specialized features like progressive overload for strength training. Its one-size-fits-all approach appeals mainly to users focused on weight loss rather than those looking for advanced workout tracking.  
4. **Apple Fitness App** This app primarily tracks calories burned and step counts, making it more suited for cardio users. It lacks the capability to log detailed weightlifting metrics, such as sets and reps, thus limiting its appeal to serious weightlifters.

### **How Our Product is Different**

Our fitness website takes a different approach by integrating both workout and nutrition tracking, addressing the needs of a broader audience, including bodybuilders, powerlifters, CrossFitters, beginners, and fitness coaches. Key differentiators include:

* **Detailed Nutrition Tracking**: Unlike competitors, our platform tracks food intake, macronutrients, and caloric consumption.  
* **Progressive Overload System**: This feature promotes strength gains by adjusting weights and reps based on performance.  
* **Customizable Workouts**: Users can create workout plans, themes, and templates, enhancing personalization.  
* **Comprehensive Features**: Pre-made workout plans help beginners get started without overthinking their routines, making it accessible for all fitness levels.
