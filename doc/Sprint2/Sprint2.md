### Acceptance Criteria
By the end of Sprint 2, we plan to implement a system that
- Creates an environment for users to be able to log their exercise routines
- Follow nutritional guidelines to ensure users are getting a safe and appropriate caloric window to meet their fitness goals
- Allow users to access the caloric intake system to track their progress and make adjustments as needed
